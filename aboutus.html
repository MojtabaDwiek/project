About us

Worktales was founded in response to a hiring market that often prioritizes speed over understanding. We saw talented people overlooked and organizations struggling with roles that looked right on paper but failed in practice. The gap was not skill, it was alignment, and that gap consistently led to missed potential on both sides.

We built Worktales around a simple belief: hiring works best when it starts with clarity. By taking the time to understand how organizations operate and what individuals bring beyond their CVs, we create matches that make sense culturally, professionally, and over time. This approach allows teams to perform better and individuals to grow with intention.

Today, Worktales partners with organizations and talent to build lasting working relationships. Every placement is treated as part of a larger journey, not a one-off decision, ensuring each role becomes a meaningful chapter in a broader work story.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Worktales handle payroll and salary management?

We manage payroll on behalf of employers, ensuring accurate payments, compliance with Lebanese labor law, and full transparency in deductions, benefits, and reporting.

Who is the legal employer when hiring through Worktales?

Depending on the model, Worktales can act as the Employer of Record (EOR) or facilitate the employment directly with the client. This ensures legal compliance and reduces risk for international companies.

How does Worktales ensure compliance with Lebanese labor law and international hiring standards?

We manage contracts, labor obligations, NDAs, taxation, and documentation, while ensuring all local and international regulations are met.

Why should an international company hire through Worktales instead of using traditional recruiters or freelancer platforms?

We combine talent quality, cultural alignment, and legal safeguards offering full payroll support, compliance management, and structured post hire support that standard recruiters don’t provide.

What cost advantages do employers gain by hiring through Lebanon?

Companies typically save 30 to 50% compared to hiring in GCC, Europe, or North America, while gaining highly skilled, multilingual professionals without relocation or high overhead.

What post-hire support does Worktales provide?

We offer onboarding assistance, training resources, performance follow-ups, and a placement guarantee to ensure long-term success and retention.

What benefits does Worktales manage for employees?

Depending on the engagement, we handle health insurance, social security, paid leave, payroll, onboarding, and administrative support on behalf of employers.

How does Worktales price its services?

We provide flexible pricing models including commission-based hiring, retainers, and managed team models. Our 50/50 structure allows cost sharing and reduced upfront risk for clients.

What makes Worktales different from other agencies in Lebanon or the region?

We operate as a talent partner, not just a recruiter combining cultural intelligence, coaching expertise, compliance infrastructure, and deep understanding of how real teams work.

How fast can Worktales deliver candidates or a shortlist?

Our average time from job brief to shortlist is significantly faster than traditional agencies, thanks to pre-vetted talent communities and structured search processes.

Does Worktales require companies to set up a legal entity in Lebanon?

No. Through our Employer of Record (EOR) model, companies can hire in Lebanon without creating a local entity.

Do you offer confidentiality and data protection?

Yes. All client and candidate data is handled securely in alignment with GDPR and Lebanese data regulations, with NDAs available upon request.
