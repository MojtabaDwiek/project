How We Work

Worktales operates as a partner. We share process visibility, configure hiring pipelines to your needs, and move in sync with your business.

Embedded Understanding

We collaborate with leadership and hiring managers to understand how teams collaborate, how decisions are made, and what the real work looks like.

Human + AI

AI expands the signal while human insight keeps nuance. We identify people who can contribute with clarity, maturity, and context.

Transparent Pipelines

You have full visibility into the process through a shared, configurable hiring pipeline that keeps alignment and accountability clear.

This ensures alignment, transparency, and momentum at every step. In short: we work with you, not for you — to build teams that last.

Core Engagements

Three service lines that flex to your chapter while keeping clarity on ownership, outcomes, and collaboration.

01

Recruitment & Executive Search

We partner with organizations to hire people who work well in their context — not just people who look good on paper.

How It Works

  1. Context & Role Understanding — define environment, expectations, and success conditions.
  2. Success Profile — outline outcomes, responsibilities, and what strong performance looks like.
  3. Sourcing & Screening — AI expands the universe; human insight selects genuine fits.
  4. Conversations That Matter — explore how candidates think, collaborate, and respond to real work demands.
  5. Shortlist Report — narrative insights including working style and discussion points.
  6. Pipeline Collaboration — review, discuss, and prioritize candidates with transparency.
  7. Offer & Transition Support — ensure clarity and alignment for a strong start.

Outcome: A considered hire that aligns with how your team works and where it is heading.

02

Remote Delivery Centers

Full-time teams in Lebanon that operate as a natural extension of your structure without relocation or restructuring.

How It Works

  1. Team Needs & Collaboration Mapping — align on outcomes, rituals, and ways of working.
  2. Role Expectations — define responsibilities and what strong contribution looks like.
  3. Talent Sourcing — AI and market intelligence widen search; we filter for maturity, working style, and fit.
  4. Team Selection Report — recommendations tied to your environment and goals.
  5. Integration Support — align workflows, tools, communication cadence, and managerial rhythms.
  6. Performance Relationship Management — we stay close to support clarity, communication, and continuity.

Important: We support performance quality and alignment, while you retain full control of deliverables and outcomes.

Outcome: A stable, connected, accountable team built where talent thrives.

03

Employer of Record (EOR)

You lead the work, we handle employment. Hire talent in Lebanon without setting up a local entity.

We Manage

  • Employment contracts
  • Payroll and payments (local or foreign currency)
  • Social security and compliance
  • Benefits administration
  • Local HR documentation

You Manage

  • The work
  • The direction
  • The performance

Outcome: Hire quickly, cleanly, and legally — with full operational clarity.
